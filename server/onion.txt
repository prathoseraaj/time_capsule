# How to Get Your .onion Link for Dead Note - Time Capsule

1. Configure Tor Hidden Service
   In your torrc file, add:
   HiddenServiceDir /path/to/hidden_service/
   HiddenServicePort 80 127.0.0.1:5000

2. Restart Tor
   sudo systemctl restart tor
   or restart the Tor service in your OS.

3. Find Your .onion Address
   After restarting, check:
   /path/to/hidden_service/hostname
   Copy the .onion address inside.
