# How to get your .onion link for Dead Note - Time Capsule:

1. Configure your Tor hidden service in your torrc file:

   HiddenServiceDir /path/to/hidden_service/
   HiddenServicePort 80 127.0.0.1:5000

2. Restart Tor (`sudo systemctl restart tor` or restart the Tor service on your OS).

3. After Tor restarts, open the file:
   /path/to/hidden_service/hostname

4. Copy the .onion address you find there and paste it below.
   Example:
   deadnoteexample12345.onion
